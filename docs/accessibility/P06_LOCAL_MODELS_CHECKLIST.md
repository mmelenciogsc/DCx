# P06 human screen-reader checklist — local models

Status: NOT RUN. Automated UIA evidence does not satisfy this checklist.

Automated evidence proves that cooperating DCx processes serialize Ollama work, recapture current resource availability, verify ordinary model unload, and make queued acquisition cancellable and recoverable. It does not prove the screen-reader wording, resource-denial discoverability, wait-state discoverability or cancellation experience when another DCx operation owns the model.

Run separately with the installed JAWS, NVDA and Narrator versions. Use only an already-installed nonprivate fixture model and the gated synthetic transfer fixture; do not perform a real provider transfer, install, retag or upload anything.

1. Keyboard to **Local models** and confirm the window title, heading, introductory no-download notice and current vision/embedding/transcription choices are announced once and in a useful order.
2. Confirm **Refresh installed models**, the identity list, complete license field, approval checkbox, role actions, clear actions and **Close** are reachable with visible focus.
3. Select a mutable/duplicate identity and confirm its exact name/digest, size, capabilities, license summary and ineligibility are understandable. Confirm neither role action permits saving it.
4. Select an eligible digest-qualified identity. Read the complete license field with normal screen-reader review commands. Confirm the exact model and license digests can be reviewed without character loss.
5. Confirm approval starts unchecked. Invoke a role action before approval and verify a concise status directs focus to approval without changing the selection.
6. Check approval, save vision, and confirm the status and current-selection text identify the exact saved binding. Confirm approval resets unchecked.
7. Repeat for an eligible embedding model if an approved one exists; otherwise record the unavailable control/state without installing one.
8. Clear each role independently and confirm the model itself is not deleted or changed.
9. Stop Ollama, refresh, and confirm the failure is announced without exception text, stale approval or an automatic remote fallback. Restart only the pre-existing service after the check.
10. Open a local video with no vision choice, invoke frame description and confirm playback stays paused while the app explains how to select a model. With the approved vision binding, confirm processing, result and manual resume are usable.
11. Repeat at 200% scaling, Windows High Contrast and keyboard-only operation. Record screen reader/app/Windows versions, failures, unexpected verbosity, focus loss and workarounds.
12. In the already-installed Whisper group, confirm executable, model and two license path fields plus all browse/review/save/clear actions are keyboard reachable with distinct access keys and useful labels. Do not select or install an unapproved backend.
13. With the deterministic fake CLI fixture only, confirm approval begins unchecked and disabled until review. Review the four files; read both complete license texts, all four digests, sizes and the warning that identity/accuracy are not established.
14. Invoke save before approval and confirm the live status refuses it and moves focus to approval. Approve and save; confirm approval resets, the current transcription binding is announced, and no download/install action exists.
15. Change any reviewed path and confirm approval/review are invalidated. Clear registration and confirm every selected executable/model/license file remains on disk.
16. With an approved real backend, open an admitted WAV in **Files and media**. Confirm **Transcribe media** pauses playback, announces processing, keeps **Cancel transcription** available, and focuses the completed read-only transcript without reading process diagnostics.
17. Cancel a long transcription and confirm no partial text remains. Clear the Whisper registration and invoke transcription again; confirm the unavailable guidance is announced and stale transcript text is absent.
18. In the FFmpeg group, review an approved fixture executable and complete license. Confirm exact digests, size, real Windows signature status and the publisher/legal warning are announced; approval begins unchecked and save-before-approval fails closed.
19. Save and clear the fixture binding. Confirm approval resets, the current media-processor identity/status is announced, source files remain, and no download/install/bundle action exists. Do not approve the real unsigned host candidate without owner/legal review.
20. With separately approved real Whisper and FFmpeg bindings, repeat **Transcribe media** for admitted MP3 and MP4 fixtures. Confirm extraction and transcription are separately announced, cancellation is available throughout, the transcript receives focus, and the media remains paused. Clear FFmpeg while retaining Whisper and confirm MP3/MP4 fail with concise registration guidance while direct WAV remains available.
21. Open **Local model benchmark review**. Confirm backend availability, manifest path, Browse, Review, approval, Run, Cancel, Close, exact-review text, aggregate-result text and assertive status are announced in a useful order and reachable by keyboard. Confirm the action access keys are distinct.
22. With no approved Whisper backend, review a transcription manifest and confirm the exact suite can be inspected while approval and Run remain unavailable. Confirm no zero score or implied pass is announced.
23. With only the generated fake backend and fixture, review a strict transcription manifest. Confirm manifest/backend/suite/policy digests, provenance, license, fixture count, WER/CER and resource thresholds are understandable. Confirm approval begins unchecked and Run remains disabled.
24. Approve and run once. Confirm Cancel remains reachable during processing; the completed aggregate result receives focus; approval resets; and the UI explains that production selection was unchanged. Change the manifest path and confirm review/approval are revoked.
25. Cancel the long generated fixture. Confirm the cancellation status is announced, no partial result appears and the prior completed aggregate evidence remains. Do not treat this generated pass as real speech accuracy.
26. With no approved embedding model, review an embedding manifest and confirm its exact digests and Recall@K/MRR/resource thresholds are readable while approval and Run remain unavailable. Confirm the missing-model guidance is concise.
27. With a separately approved real embedding model, confirm review announces the exact backend digest and Ollama runtime version, approval begins unchecked, and Run becomes available only after approval. After a run, confirm the focused aggregate result labels Ollama values as loaded memory/GPU memory, announces unload confirmation, revokes approval and leaves the production selection unchanged.
28. In **Approved dependency transfer — inert storage only**, read the exact plan and startup deny-all redirect-policy SHA-256. Confirm **Observe current provider routes** explains that it sends public HEAD requests, reads no body or payload, retains no signed query value, is not transfer approval, and binds a successful review only to the displayed plan so a changed plan is denied before payload contact or storage creation. Confirm transfer approval and **Begin approved transfer** remain unavailable until a current in-memory route review exists. Do not invoke the real action during this checklist without separate owner authorization.
29. In the gated synthetic fixture, confirm the route-review and cancel controls have distinct labels/access keys and remain safely unavailable because the fixture makes zero provider requests. Confirm approval enables the synthetic transfer once. Verify item ordinal and byte progress are understandable without excessive repeated speech, **Cancel transfer** remains reachable, and other transfer/review actions are unavailable during work.
30. Cancel the first synthetic attempt. Confirm the cleanup result receives focus, no partial completion is implied, and the one-time approval resets. Approve and complete a second attempt; confirm the protected/reverified result receives focus, progress reaches 100%, and approval resets again.
31. Invoke **Verify completed set** and **Recover abandoned partials** by keyboard. Confirm each concise result receives focus. Confirm recovery explains that links are refused and no completed set was changed.
32. Repeat the synthetic workflow at 200% scaling and High Contrast. Check `Alt+O`, `Alt+K`, `Alt+B`, `Alt+C`, `Alt+V` and `Alt+R` for conflicts with each screen reader. Record verbosity, delayed announcements, cancellation latency and any focus loss. Do not infer this human result from automated UIA.
33. With a disposable validation profile, start one long local-model operation and initiate a second operation from another DCx process. Confirm the waiting state does not claim progress or completion, cancellation remains discoverable, cancellation produces a concise announcement, and a later retry works after the first process releases or exits. Do not use real user media and do not start an unrelated Ollama client during the resource-attribution check.
34. On an NVIDIA host, review the hardware summary. Confirm the adapter's reported total memory, current free memory and `NVIDIA NVML free memory` source are distinguishable and understandable without character-by-character review. On a host without supported live GPU telemetry, confirm the unavailable GPU-admission explanation is concise and does not imply that registry-reported capacity is currently usable.
35. Using only a generated resource-denial fixture, confirm that a model which becomes ineligible before inference produces one concise status, retains focus and does not claim that processing started. After a successful generated vision or embedding operation, confirm the completion status is not announced until exact-model unload has been verified; an unconfirmed cleanup fixture must announce failure rather than the otherwise usable answer.

P06 remains blocked until this human gate and the separate real Whisper/embedding gates pass.
